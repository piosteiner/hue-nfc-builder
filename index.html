Hue NFC Command Builder

Tap your phone on an NFC tag and your Hue lights switch. This page builds everything your phone's automation app needs for that, step by step.

You need: your Hue bridge's IP address · an API key (step 1 shows how to get one) · an NFC tag · an automation app on your phone (step 5).

An independent, unofficial tool. I'm not affiliated with Philips, Signify or Philips Hue, and this is not a Hue product.

Why the detour through a tab?

Browsers don't let a website talk to devices in your home network in the background, and phones offer no way to allow it. Opening a page yourself is fine. Your tag isn't affected: your app talks to the bridge directly.

On a computer you can also try this: open your bridge over https, accept the certificate warning, come back and tap “Load from bridge”.

No API key yet? Get one in a minute
  1. On your home Wi-Fi, open http://<bridge IP>/debug/clip.html. That's a page on your bridge.
  2. Into URL type /api. Into Message Body paste {"devicetype":"nfc_tags#phone"}
  3. Press the big round button on top of your bridge, then tap POST within 30 seconds.
  4. In the answer (Command Response), copy the long code after "username": and paste it above as your API key.

Keep the key private: anyone in your home network who has it can switch your lights.

Paste your bridge data by hand

Paste the answer of /api/<API key> (everything), or of /lights and /groups one after the other: they're merged.

4 Preview

Select a light or room to see it here.

Each lamp stands where that kind of lamp usually does: strips behind the sofa, floor and table lamps at the sides, the rest on the ceiling. Each light does only what it can: a white bulb ignores a colour, a colour bulb shows the closest colour it can make. A screen can't show light exactly, so take it as a close guide.

5 Set it up in your app

Your app

Not an ad: I have no connection to these apps and get nothing from them. Any app that reacts to NFC tags and sends web requests works. Prices can change.

Select at least one light or room.

Runs entirely in your browser. Your bridge IP, username, light list and selection are kept only in this browser's storage; the page sends nothing anywhere except the requests you trigger, straight to your bridge. No tracking, nothing loaded from other sites.
