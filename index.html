Hue NFC Command Builder

Pick lights or rooms, choose what should happen, preview it, and copy the request into the automation app that reads your NFC tag.

An independent, unofficial tool. I'm not affiliated with Philips, Signify or Philips Hue, and this is not a Hue product.

1 Your bridge

No username yet?
  1. Open http://<bridge-ip>/debug/clip.html in a browser on your home Wi-Fi.
  2. URL /api, body {"devicetype":"nfc_tags#phone"}.
  3. Press the round button on the bridge, then POST within 30 seconds.
  4. The response contains "username": "…": that's your key. Keep it private; anyone on your network with it can control your lights.
…or paste the JSON you got from the CLIP debugger

Paste the response of /api/<username>/lights, /groups, or the full /api/<username>. You can paste lights and groups one after the other: they get merged.

4 Preview

Select a light or room to see it here.

Where your lamps really are, the page can't know: a lightstrip glows behind the sofa, floor and table lamps stand where they'd usually be, everything else hangs from the ceiling. Each light does only what it can do: a white bulb ignores a colour, colour bulbs only reach the colours their hardware can make. A screen can't show light exactly, so take it as a close guide.

5 Requests for your tag

Your app

Any app that can react to an NFC tag and send a web request (PUT with a JSON body) works; these are just common ones. This is no advertisement: I have no connection to any of them and get nothing from them. Prices and limits can change, so check the app store.

Select at least one light or room.

Runs entirely in your browser. Your bridge IP, username, light list and selection are kept only in this browser's storage; the page sends nothing anywhere except the requests you trigger, straight to your bridge. No tracking, nothing loaded from other sites.
